---
title: Epilogue
part: The nights that followed
---

What Jerome had offered Susanna turned out to be real. The major boon still hung over her. But
night to night, life with the Gangrel was amiable, and they defended their territory hard. There
were rumours of other beasts in the woods nearby. So far, nothing had come out of them.

---

Nico became a major Anarch player, all the more because he had connections and could deal
directly with the Sabbat and with the Camarilla both. He got a name as a dealbroker. He was very
curious about AURORA, which had gone quiet. The attack at the Fairmont had cost them, and drawn
the Sabbat's attention. Nico watched them, and made sure what they did stayed inside the bounds he
thought acceptable.

---

Claire was in a delicate position. She had walked away from the mess with the Sabbat, but the
Sabbat had not been dealt with, and Kamal was upset about it. And she had failed to bring Susanna
back, which made her place with Michel and the Camarilla delicate too.

She reached out to Susanna, and tried to tempt her with knowledge, with blood sorcery. But Claire
still worked under Michel, and so whatever passed between them, and whatever trust, went only so
far.

She had worked the ritual, and worked it in a way that until then had only been a theory. That
raised her in Michel's esteem. It also made him press her harder to bring Susanna in.

---

Richart was satisfied with how it had ended. He was thankful it had not come, in the end, to
extermination.

---

Kamal was stuck. The big push for a Camarilla takeover had had to wait, and so he waited. He still
resented Claire, a little, for shrinking back when it mattered.
