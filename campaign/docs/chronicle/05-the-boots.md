---
title: Follow the Boots
part: Sunday, 13 June 2021
---

Susanna woke to find one of Baruch's dossiers waiting for her, with a note from Richart. She
opened it and read.

Baruch had been shopping her and Estelle to all three factions. A bidding war, of a kind: the
most safety for them, the biggest cut for him, in boons and in intelligence. Glaurung had been his
contact for the Sabbat. Gisele for the Camarilla. Jerome for the Anarchs.

Claire fed from one of the blood bags, and then looked at Susanna with interest.

---

Nico wanted them to come on a vision journey with him. He went out alone to feed first.

They met at Bar le Sacrilège and took a private room. Nico and Susanna went under together.
Claire stood watch.

Nico's vision was a strong one, and it was about AURORA. They had been working from Camarilla
intelligence, and it had come from Gisele Singh, the seneschal.

He came out of it in a panic. "Get your hands off me, hands off me."

"That was disturbing," Susanna said. "Nico, it wasn't real."

Claire had frozen, not knowing what to do.

When he had calmed down, Nico said, "We need to reach out to our friends, they're in Sabbat
territory."

Did he have a contact, Claire asked, or a lead?

"We need to follow the boots."

---

They decided to go back to the mausoleum and take the wight to the Sabbat, as a peace offering.

They commandeered an Uber and loaded the staked wight into the back, and Nico directed the driver
to the alley behind Sybille's haven. He went in first, and left the carrying of the wight to
Claire and Susanna, who struggled in after him.

Nico introduced Claire and Susanna as his friends, and the wight as his dog.

Claire realised where she was, and went pale. She introduced herself civilly, and hoped nobody
knew who she was.

Nico left the wight with Sybille. He would train it with her, he said; it was not simply hers to
do anything she liked with. Claire excused herself, took Susanna, and went back to the mausoleum.

Not long after they left, the Spaniard arrived. Nico asked him about AURORA, and the Spaniard
hatched a plan. A gift of coordinates, from the Sabbat bishops to the Camarilla, for the
Camarilla to pass to AURORA. But Nico was to swap them for the coordinates of the Prince's haven,
and set the court and AURORA on each other.

Nico went back to the mausoleum.

---

Back at the haven, Claire confessed. She had given away the secret of Susanna's blood to a
superior whose intentions were hard to read and rarely kind. She did not know how to protect
Susanna now, and she had put her in more danger. She was sorry.

Susanna was crushed. Claire suggested they find a way to turn it aside.

Nico came in. He complimented Susanna's thigh-high purple sparkly boots, and told them the
mustache had told him to tell the Prince where to go.

Claire was worried, and more so when she learned it had been a Sabbat mustache. In the end she
agreed to take the coordinates to Gisele. Nico would stay with Susanna. He gave Claire one of his
two pieces of paper, and did not check which set of coordinates it was.

---

While Claire was gone, Nico found something to feed Sunshine, and Susanna went to talk to Jerome.
Nico went with her.

Susanna told Jerome she knew he and Baruch had been discussing particulars. In the end they
agreed: Susanna would have a home under the protection of the Gangrel, the Quebecois Anarch
motorcycle gang, in exchange for a major boon. Matthieu would be told to find her at the mausoleum.

---

Claire met Gisele. The paper, she explained, was information about where the Sabbat would be,
a meeting perhaps, and it had come by way of Nico. She suggested Gisele ask the Prince for the
context. Gisele thanked her, and dismissed her.

A text went to a number Gisele was known to watch.

*There is a trap for the Camarilla at the set of coordinates that Nico possesses, not the ones
Claire passed to the Prince.*

---

Nico, Claire and Susanna went out to a club. They persuaded the bouncer that Sunshine was a cat,
and allowed on the premises, and not a dog.

Nico offered Susanna the mausoleum as a home, so the "cat" could stay. It could play with the
"dog", he said, the wight, once he had it trained.

They went over what Nico and Susanna had each seen in the vision. Tendrils across the city.
Nerve clusters. There had been two of those, and Claire recognised one of them: a warehouse that
matched Susanna's description of the place where she had been held.
