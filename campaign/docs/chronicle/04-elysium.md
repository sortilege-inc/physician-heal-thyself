---
title: Elysium
part: Saturday, 12 June 2021
---

The next night Kamal had himself put right, and felt less like jelly. Claire's leg mended a little
more. Richart healed.

On the way, Kamal had a question for Richart. Would he know a man of his own clan with something
vaguely Danish about his face?

Richart knew who that was. Glaurung. A Tzimisce, and a bishop; he and Milica were the two still
left in the city. And this was the Kindred Susanna was bound to.

Richart was to deliver the package to the Prince. Did anyone know what was in it? Could anyone lift
a seal and put it back? A couple of them thought they might. But Richart was already going into very
dangerous waters as a Tzimisce, and he did not want to hand the Camarilla a reason to kill him on
sight for tampering with what he carried. He left it as it was.

Richart had a good twenty pairs of cufflinks, none of them flashy. He had given Nico three hundred
dollars all the same. Nico gave him cufflinks that had been very fashionable five years ago, on the
theory that Richart would never know the difference: silver rectangles, each with a green emerald.
Richart wore them, with a forest green shirt, a dark grey tie and a fitted charcoal suit. He looked
as dapper as he could, and very young. He and Nico were of an age to look at, early twenties, and
between them they looked like two frat boys who had cleaned up nice. Borrowed dad's suit.

---

Claire did not trust Susanna at the Elysium. She trusted her even less alone; the last time Susanna
had been alone, she had been abducted. So Claire took her to the chantry instead, to show it to her.

Nico came on his own, with a flask, in a black-tie tuxedo, obsidian cufflinks and buttons, and a
little red on the soles of his shoes.

He came first. Anselm, the Prince's herald, a masked Nosferatu, announced each guest as they came,
red-carpet style, and then they walked on in. Inside it was relaxed. There were tall, thin glasses of
any blood a guest might want, carried round by ghouls in beautiful dresses and tuxedos. It was small
and close: fewer than thirty vampires, fifty people perhaps with the staff, done up like a cosy,
exquisite wedding reception.

The Toreador knew how to present themselves, and Mohamed had put on a phenomenal show. Nico was
introduced to Maggie Molyneux, a stunning redhead, primogen of the Toreador. They talked fashion,
and the arts, and she introduced him to others, and nudged him, not subtly, to throw his lot in with
them and stop all this independence nonsense.

---

Kamal and Richart came together. Kamal wore a suit of the sort a man could buy off the rack at a
decent store, and no tie.

He looked for Javier and found him partly in shadow, in a very professional suit, with an earpiece
and his hair tied up in a bun, running some kind of security detail. Javier gave him a nod.

The room had a natural centre, and at it stood Prince Beatrice and Gisele and a few others, talking
as if casually. The formal part of the night had not started. If Richart wanted to know whether he
was on the agenda, the Keeper of Elysium was the one to ask. Business before pleasure.

Mohamed greeted him. "Welcome. I hear that you're here representing the Sabbat."

"That is incorrect. But the welcome is nevertheless appreciated. I do have a missive from that
organization that I was instructed to deliver to the Prince. Should I do that now? Or is there some
sort of ceremony that should be observed?"

"If it isn't urgent, then we'll account for that as part of our proceedings tonight. Please do feel
free to feed as necessary. There are drinks that should service any and all tastes. And please know
that we do abide by a certain discretion at these events. But you are under full diplomatic
protection while you're here."

"It is appreciated."

Richart drifted off towards the drinks. He was hungry, and he kept them coming, and kept up the frat
boy act.

Kamal went to Mohamed and asked for a guest list.

"Do give me a few moments, but I can obtain one for you shortly. I have provided Javier with a full
list for review previously. Is there a security concern?"

There was not, said Kamal.

"Wonderful."

Kamal did not expect them to serve anything he could drink here, and his hunger was low enough. He
had a pocket bleach stick with him, the rub-on kind, in case his stigmata got out of hand.

---

At the chantry, Claire was curious.

"When you described the Salubri, and what you were trying to do, it's a lot different than what
I've been told for ever. I also thought that your clan was completely exterminated, and the ones
that were left were scattered to the wind. So I admit I'm a bit curious. And your blood potency is so
crazy, off the charts. I'm just a bit intrigued about your lineage, and what you were up to at
Baruch's."

Susanna knew some version of the story: that the head of the Salubri had been destroyed by the ones
who became the Tremere, and that they had climbed to their place in the Camarilla over as many
Salubri as they could exterminate.

"Actually, the blood potency, as far as I know, is one of the reasons why we have been hunted, and
not quite to extinction. But yes, there are very few of us. I'll decide to trust you, and tell you
that, yes, there is this history of animosity between our clans. But I'm still pretty new at this.
What are those differences you mentioned? What's so surprising to you?"

"We were always taught that you participated in a heightened form of diablerie. You were sort of
boogeymen, rather than what you're known for, the Golconda. Well, you certainly don't seem like a
huge threat. But you definitely have some kind of immense power in your blood. So I feel like you
should be protected."

"Thanks. I appreciate that."

"Which is why I don't think we should go to the Elysium. People there might know a lot more than me.
It could be bad."

---

At the Elysium the evening came to order. There was housekeeping first; the two primogens gave brief
reports. Then Javier stepped forward to speak to the security of the domain.

He told them about an attack on an Anarch haven, brought to his attention by Kamal and by Claire, who
was not present. Two unaffiliated vampires, meaning not Camarilla, had met the Final Death, presumably
at the hands of the Sabbat. It was an escalation of needless conflict and a risk to the Masquerade,
and it only showed why the Camarilla needed to step up and take the city back. If the Prince called a
blood hunt on a vampire who deserved it, that was one thing. This was reckless, and unsafe for
everyone.

He described the scene as he had found it, and the reckless use of technology there, the cameras.
This was why they kept the values they kept, the strict rules against modern technology, the social
order they had. It was part report, part polemic. That it was the Sabbat, he asserted; he offered
nothing to show it that they had not seen for themselves.

He called Kamal forward.

Kamal thanked him. "I don't have any further detail to disclose at this time, but the investigation
is ongoing. And I would thank everyone here for their cooperation."

He sat down. Gisele thanked him on the Prince's behalf. The Prince only listened.

"We have a presentation," Mohamed announced, "on behalf of the Sabbat, from Richart Cartier."

Richart held the package in both hands and came forward slowly, giving anyone every chance to
intercept him. He did not kneel. He held it out.

Prince Beatrice stepped forward and took it with a slight bow of her head. "Thank you for providing
this."

She opened it. A book, and a sheaf of papers, letters, with a note on the first. She handed the set
to Gisele, read the first letter quietly to herself, and handed that over as well.

"Thank you. Please feel free to enjoy yourself for the remainder of the evening. But do know that
this will not be a recurring invitation."

"I will try to withstand my devastation."

She gave him a forced smile, and he was excused.

There was a commotion at the door.

"We are welcoming," Anselm called, "the unaffiliated, Jerome."

Jerome was strolling in before the words were out. Nico took the flask from his pocket and drank very
deeply at the sight of him, and put it back. Jerome walked straight for the Prince and her circle.
Richart went and lurked in a corner near an exit.

---

Claire had FOMO. She did not know how easily anyone there might see through a disguise, but the
Elysium mattered, and there was not much to do at the chantry.

Would Susanna be afraid to go? Nobody would sense her blood, as far as Susanna knew, unless they had a
power for it and used it. And she had hidden her eye from mortals already. She had grown out great big
bangs. Claire gave her a head covering, a sash across the forehead under a hat, like an old film
star's turban.

They came late. Claire talked their way past being announced; she did not want to disturb the
proceedings. They slipped in just after Jerome had made his entrance.

Jerome was not in the least put out by the disturbance he was making. He walked up towards the Prince
and then turned hard left instead, to Michel. Michel's childe, he said, was delinquent in her payment,
and as such that payment was now deemed to be Michel's.

Michel was blindsided, and furious, and held himself in check, because this was Elysium. Jerome was
surely mistaken.

---

Kamal and Nico both saw through the disguises at once. Richart was trying to catch every word between
Jerome and Michel.

Business had paused. Kamal made his way to Javier.

"I wanted to apprise you of our investigation, and I have a request as well. I'm waiting on the guest
list. That's my first note. My second is, I actually am not sure that the Sabbat should be our first
suspect. I am concerned that it's mortals. I'm concerned about the Masquerade."

He felt Javier press into his mind. He held it off, but he felt the weight of it, as Javier said:
"We are sure that the Sabbat are responsible, and they will be appropriately punished for it."

Kamal understood. By far the biggest threat to the Camarilla's power in the city was the Sabbat, the
real rulers of much of Quebec City. Whether or not the Sabbat had done this, blaming them was useful.
Baruch had not been well liked, but he had been truly neutral, and something like this could win the
Camarilla the Anarchs' sympathy, or at least their non-interference, in a push against the Sabbat.
However the facts fell, it was to the Camarilla's advantage that this be a Sabbat attack.

He had nothing to gain here. He demurred. "I understand. I'll continue the investigation and keep you
apprised."

As he walked away, Mohamed came up and handed him a small folder. "This is what was provided to Javier
earlier. It should be the full list of invitations. I don't have it cross-referenced with the full
list of attendees, but it should be close to the same."

"Thank you."

Kamal took it to a corner.

---

Susanna had never been to an Elysium. She was curious, and she opened her senses to the room. There
were wards painted into the decorations on the walls, part of the security. She did not know what
kind. Everyone who was not staff was Kindred, and the staff were all ghouls, carrying tall glasses of
blood of every resonance.

"Wow, Claire, this is quite the affair here. Is this pretty typical, for events like this that you've
been to in the past?"

"At this moment, not particularly."

Claire was ducking behind people as she talked, trying to stay out of Jerome's sight. It was normally a
dressy affair, she said. She did not come very often. She did not really go in for the fashion, and
did not have the time.

Susanna asked whether she could help her hide from him, and who he was, and what the deal was.

"If we keep our eyes out for Richart or Kamal, possibly Nico. Let's aim for someone I might be able to
hand you off to. And then I could get out of here."

Susanna saw Kamal, and went to him, so that Claire would be less obvious without her.

---

Michel had locked eyes with Claire and was coming for her. She was his only acknowledged childe here.
She let him see she had seen him, and went into a hallway, out of sight of the room. He caught up with
her there.

"So is that why you stood up the event? Or are hiding your face? What have you gotten us into?"

"It was a good plan. I felt like it was a solid plan, and I sort of dug myself in a little too deep
with Jerome, and my plan fizzled. But I have plans to make up for it. I was intending on giving him a
little more time, and giving him something that would make up for all of this."

"Your plans seem to have involved standing up and withholding payment on a major boon to a man that you
should never have owed in the first place. Why could you not have simply asked? We could have
coordinated something."

"It was my own. It was a really good plan, I thought, and it was going to be a surprise, really. I
thought it would be an awesome thing. But I know how to fix this. I have a plan."

"Give me one good reason why we shouldn't just hand you over to Jerome now."

Claire took out the vial of blood she had kept.

"What's that?"

"Just taste a drop."

She gave him a drop, and kept the vial. He was confused and did not understand, and he took a lick,
and his eyes lit up with astonishment.

"Is that what I think it is?"

"Yes, I think so. And I don't know how it exists here, but—"

"And there's more where that came from?"

"Yes. Yes."

"Don't tell Jerome of this. Let me speak to the Prince, and we can find a way to get him off your back.
Stay. Give me ten minutes. I'll get him removed from the Elysium, and then you can drop this mask. I
don't know how you managed that, but that is a coup."

He went. Claire breathed out, and leaned against the wall of the hallway.

---

Susanna came up to Kamal while he was going through the papers. Glaurung was not on the list. Jerome
was. So was Baruch, scratched out. So was Estelle, scratched out too.

Susanna saw her sire's name. "Wow. Okay. My sire was originally invited to this. And I'm also really
curious about these wards on the walls."

Kamal had not been looking for them, but he knew enough blood sorcery to read them quickly. Wards
against ghouls and against spirits, to keep out trouble of either kind. Standard, he imagined, at
events like these.

"Are those the same kind of things Nico was looking at on the wall earlier?"

"It's never perfectly clear what Nico means at any given moment. It might well be this. It might be
the cameras." He looked at her. "What are you doing here? I saw you come in, but I didn't hear your
name. Did you sneak in? You're not on the guest list. Did Estelle intend to have you here?"

"Yes, I believe Estelle intended to have me here. Claire and I decided to come in without any fanfare,
just for safety's sake."

Jerome's name surprised him; Jerome was unaffiliated. Though if important Anarchs were going to turn
up anywhere, it would be an Elysium.

The Prince was hearing petitions now. The petitioner and the Prince and a small audience attended to
each one; around the edges, everyone else went back to their own conversations.

"Well, frankly, we came to be seen," said Kamal. "A couple of us came to be heard, and we were heard. I
don't think we should overstay our time here."

---

Michel came back out of the side room, took Jerome by the elbow for what was going to be a very stern
conversation, and walked him out of the Elysium.

Richart could not follow. He went to find Kamal, and only when he was close did he see that the woman
with him was Susanna.

He spoke under his breath. "What on earth is Susanna doing here?"

"It's Susanna. I came here with Claire. She's trying to avoid notice by Jerome, and the best way for her
to do that was to come here. I don't know why you're so suspicious of me, man. I'm just trying to get
my bearings in this crazy new world."

"I am not angry at you. You obviously are not the one who should know better."

Gisele came across and touched his shoulder. "The Prince has a response for you. Do you have a moment?"

---

Nico was working the room, talking to as many people as he could, trying to find his way to the Prince.

He noticed a ghoul standing behind the Prince and off to one side. It had come up and spoken to her
earlier, and then withdrawn. It looked like one of the figures from his vision. He went over and said
hello.

"Were you in my vision?"

"I'm seeing you. I assume you're seeing me as well."

"Not the vision of the now. The vision of the other time, like, on the walls, with the eyes."

"I don't know what you're talking about, sir."

"It would be very helpful if I could speak with the Prince."

"She's over there. I think you just need to get in the queue."

"I don't like lines. Could you just ask her to come over here?"

"I will attempt."

The ghoul waited for a break, and caught the Prince's attention. She looked over, nodded, and came.

"Thank you for coming over."

"Yes. Is it Nico I hear?"

"Yes, just so."

"I heard you are potentially interested in joining our endeavor."

"Endeavors I like to help out with. Joining is not really my thing. But I have a question for you, and
this might seem a bit odd. Have you had any news of the northern lights moving?"

"Yes. You're speaking of a true Aurora."

"A true Aurora. And an Aurora with many eyes and many arms, and sometimes badges. This is an endeavor
that we should all be part of."

"It's an endeavor that is a sign of power for some, and a sign of doom for others."

"Agreed. I have friends here too. I think they might be helpful. I feel like the doom might reach too
many of the Kindred. Is this a concern of yours as well?"

"I mean, too many is a bit subjective, really."

"You don't enjoy the Kindred? They're fun. They're a good time. We wouldn't want the doom to reach all
of us."

"We. I feel fairly confident that those under the Camarilla's protection will be spared."

"Then perhaps you underestimate the Aurora. It is a true Aurora."

"Let's remain in touch. But the offer of protection extends only to those who are in the Camarilla.
Simply being a guest at an Elysium is not sufficient. Good day."

"Good day. Or good evening, I guess."

The Prince went back to her duties.

---

Then Richart was brought over to her.

"Thank you. We have drafted a brief, but hopefully sufficient, response."

She gave him a package, not sealed, and asked that he deliver it unopened to Milica and Glaurung. It was
a small wooden box with a hinged lid, the size of a small novel. It fitted into the inner pocket of his
coat.

"And should I be leaving?"

"That would be our preference. But you're welcome to stay until close."

"I do appreciate the hospitality. It has been an illuminating evening."

---

Richart went to Nico.

"So what do you talk about?"

"Lights."

"Wonderful. So I believe it is in everyone's best interest for Susanna to leave, very, very quickly. I
don't suppose you could provide some sort of distraction?"

"I think I might be able to manage it."

Richart made his way through the crowd in a wide arc, never straight at Kamal and Susanna, keeping them
in view. Claire's ten minutes were up; she came back into the room from the other side, saw that Jerome
was gone, saw Richart heading for Kamal and Susanna, and went to meet them.

Nico walked a little way off from the Prince and began, horribly, to hit on everybody he could reach,
and to try and get them all drinking with him, loudly, while they were trying to do very serious things.
Soon everybody was yelling at him to shut up. Then he faked a vision, and began to scream about hands
reaching out of the ceiling. Javier came over and began to restrain him. A crowd gathered to watch, the
way people watch a car accident.

"What's happening with Nico?" said Susanna.

"It is planned," said Richart. "Don't worry. You can stay if you wish. But I believe, Susanna, it's best
that you and I get out of here while we still can."

"I have no business here anymore," said Kamal, "so I'm happy to go with you."

"Claire, we were just about to leave. Would you like to join us?"

"Yes." Claire looked at Susanna. "Feel free to take point."

"I'm ready. I've been ready to go."

---

Outside, they sorted out where to go. Susanna was reluctant to leave, but she left. She asked Claire
whether she could go back to the chantry with her.

"I have tons of sleeping quarters," said Claire. "I want to keep you out of sight. I guess this was a bad
idea, coming here in the first place."

"I believe that perhaps a wiser idea would be safety in numbers. Perhaps we should all forsake our private
havens for one more night, and stay together."

The chantry was no good for that. Kamal would not be welcome there, to be frank. The mausoleum had room
for all of them, and Calliope was still there.

The mausoleum, then. There was safety in numbers.
