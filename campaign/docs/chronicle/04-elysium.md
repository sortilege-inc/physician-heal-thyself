---
title: Elysium
part: Saturday, 12 June 2021
---

The next night Richart healed, and Claire's leg mended a little more. The changes Richart had
worked on Kamal came undone.

On the way, Kamal described what the blood walk had shown him: the Kindred that Susanna was
bound to. Richart knew that description. He suspected it was Glaurung, bishop of the Sabbat.

Richart mentioned that he was delivering a package. Kamal asked what was in it. They thought
about opening it without anyone knowing, and in the end left it as it was.

Nico produced the cufflinks: silver rectangles with an emerald at the centre. Richart wore a
four-screen shirt, a dark grey tie and a fitted charcoal suit. He looked dapper, and far too
young. The two of them looked like frat boys who had cleaned up nice.

---

Anselm, masked, announced each Kindred as they came into Elysium. Ghouls in beautiful clothes
walked the room with tall, thin, lidded glasses of blood.

Richart and Kamal arrived together. Nico was there already and enjoying himself. Claire took
Susanna to her chantry instead.

The Toreador had style, and Mohamed had outdone himself. Nico was talking to Maggie, who was
urging him to think about joining the Tower.

Kamal wore a suit and a bow tie. He saw Javier off to one side, his hair tied up in a bun. Prince
Beatrice stood at the room's natural centre, talking with Gisele and others. The formal part of
the night had not yet begun.

Richart found Mohamed and asked that the delivery of his package be put on the agenda. Mohamed
assured him that he was under Elysium's protection, and reminded him, distrustfully, of what the
Masquerade required. Kamal joined them and asked for a guest list. Mohamed said he would get him
one, though he had given one to Javier already. Was there a security concern? There was not,
Kamal said.

---

In the chantry, Claire showed Susanna around and told her something of her work. What she had
learned about the Salubri was very different, she said. That they had been all but wiped out. She
was curious about Susanna's lineage, and about blood as potent as hers.

"The blood potency is definitely part of the reason we have been hunted," said Susanna. "I'll
choose to trust you, and acknowledge that there has been animosity between our clans."

"We were told that you participated in a kind of verboten diablerie," said Claire. "You were
boogiemen, and there wasn't much discussion of success with Golconda. But I think you should be
protected, which is why I didn't bring us to the Elysium, yet."

---

The formal part of the Elysium began, and Javier opened it.

He described the destruction of an Anarch haven and of two unaffiliated Kindred, most likely by
the Sabbat, an escalation of pointless conflict that put the Masquerade at risk. It drove home how
badly the Camarilla needed to extend its reach and secure Quebec City against that kind of
reckless gamble. He described the scene, the reckless use of technology. It was part clinical
report, part polemic, part speculation.

He called Kamal forward to corroborate. Kamal thanked him, said he had nothing to add, and
stepped down. Gisele thanked Javier.

Gisele called Richart forward. He brought the package. Prince Beatrice took it, thanked him, and
opened it: a book, and a sheaf of papers. She handed the book to Gisele and read the papers, and
then she answered him.

"Thank you, and do enjoy the rest of the night, but understand that this invitation will not
recur."

Richart bowed his head. "It was not my decision."

"We welcome the unaffiliated," Anselm announced, "Jerome Giovanni."

Jerome was already strolling in as he said it, confident, straight for the Prince. Richart moved
back towards an exit.

---

Claire gave Susanna a discreet sash to cover her forehead, and they came to the Elysium late,
just after Jerome. Claire talked Anselm out of announcing them; she did not want to disturb the
proceedings.

She watched Jerome walk up to the Prince, and then swerve off to berate Michel instead: about his
childe, and a delinquent payment that was now Michel's responsibility. Michel was blindsided. He
told Jerome he was surely mistaken.

Kamal and Nico saw Claire and Susanna come in. Richart was listening to Jerome and Michel.

Kamal went back to Javier. They should not be so certain it was the Sabbat, he said. He believed
mortals had been involved.

He felt Javier's will press into his mind. "We are certain of the Sabbat's disruptive influence."

Kamal understood. Blaming the Sabbat was politically convenient, whether or not the Sabbat had
done it, and the Anarchs were unlikely to stand in the way. As he walked off, Mohamed handed him a
folder. It had not been checked against who was actually there.

Susanna opened her senses to the room. The space was warded, and the waiting staff were all
ghouls. She picked out Nico, Kamal and Richart in the crowd.

"Wow, this is quite the affair," she said. "Is this pretty typical?"

Claire was panicking. She tried to answer calmly while she kept out of Jerome's line of sight.

---

Michel's eyes found Claire, and he came straight for her. She ducked into a side room. He caught
up with her there.

"Is that why you tried to stand us up? What foolishness have you gotten us into?"

"I felt like it was a solid plan, and it fizzled, but I'm working on something that will make good
on everything."

"Your 'plans' involved standing up and withholding payment on a major boon to a kindred you should
never have owed in the first place. Why did you not simply ask? We could have coordinated
something, whatever it was."

"Well, it was going to be a surprise, an awesome thing, but I promise I'll get it handled."

"Give me one good reason why I shouldn't hand you over to Jerome right now."

Claire took out the vial of Susanna's blood.

"What's that?"

She put a drop on her finger and held it out to him. Michel did not understand. He licked it
anyway, and his eyes lit up.

"Is.... is that what I think it is?"

"Yes, I don't know how it exists here, but..."

"But there's more where that came from?"

"...yes."

"Don't speak to Jerome of this. Let me talk to the Prince. We can find a way to get him off your
back. Give me ten minutes and I'll have Jerome removed from the Elysium. I don't know how you
managed to obtain that, but it's a coup."

Claire sighed, and slumped against the wall.

---

Kamal went through the papers. Glaurung was not on the list. Jerome was. So were Baruch and
Estelle, both struck out.

Susanna saw Estelle's name and asked Kamal about it, and about the wards. Kamal knew them: wards
against ghouls and spirits.

"Was that what Nico was referring to?"

"We're never quite sure what Nico is referring to."

---

Nico noticed a ghoul standing a little apart from the Prince, and it looked like one of the
figures from his vision. He went over and said hello.

"Were you in my vision?"

"Well, I see you now."

"Not my vision now, earlier, with the walls and the eyes."

"I don't know."

"Could you call the Prince over here?"

"She seems occupied."

"Make an attempt."

The ghoul went and spoke to the Prince, and when she had finished with the petition in front of
her, she came over.

"Thank you for coming," said Beatrice. "Are you considering joining our endeavor?"

"I'm not much of a joiner. I have a question for you; have you had any news of the northern
lights, moving?"

"...yes. A true Aurora."

"Yes! With many eyes and many arms. And sometimes badges. This is an endeavor we should all be
part of."

"It's an endeavor that is a sign of power for some, doom for others."

"Agreed. I have friends here, they could be helpful, we are concerned that the doom might reach
too many of us."

"Too many is subjective."

"You don't enjoy kindred? They're a good time! You don't want the doom to reach all of us."

"I'm confident that those of the Camarilla will be spared."

"Then perhaps you underestimate the Aurora; it is a true Aurora."

"Let's remain in touch," said the Prince, "but the offer of protection is only for joiners; simply
being a guest at Elysium is insufficient. Good evening."

---

Richart saw Michel storm into the room, take Jerome by the elbow, and walk him out of it.

He went over to Kamal and Susanna and asked where Claire was. Coming here had been a terrible
idea, he said, and he did not think much of Claire for deciding it.

Gisele came up. "The Prince has a response for you, could you join us?"

The Prince came over. "We have drafted a brief but hopefully sufficient response." She gave him
an unsealed package, a small wooden box about the size of a novel, and asked him to deliver it to
Milica and Glaurung.

Richart went to Nico. "I think it's in everyone's best interests to leave with Susanna; could you
manage a distraction?"

Nico could. Richart went to get the others ready. Claire came back in from the other side of the
room and joined them.

Nico started drinking loudly and trying to get everyone around him to join in. Then he dropped his
glass and began screaming about arms reaching down out of the ceiling. Javier came over and held
him down. It was distraction enough, and the others got out.

Susanna left reluctantly. She asked Claire whether they could go back to the chantry, but in the
end they went back to the mausoleum. There was safety in numbers.
