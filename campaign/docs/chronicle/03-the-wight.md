---
title: The Wight
part: Friday, 11 June 2021
---

Susanna woke in a hunger frenzy. This was what walking in the light cost her.

The others woke to find Calliope already up, and facing something that was coming up out of the
ground. The soil and the stone of the floor were taking a shape, and the shape was a body. It was
the monstrous form of a vampire, the kind in the old pictures: long claws, fangs grown out of all
proportion, something on it that might once have been clothes. It roared, and went for Calliope,
and knocked her senseless into the wall.

Susanna knew what it was. This was a wight. This was what became of a vampire who had lost every
last shred of their humanity, the thing every one of them held on to that shred to avoid becoming.
There was nothing left in it but hunger, or desperation. And Susanna, right now, was not so very
different.

Richart knew the trick of coming up out of the earth. It belonged to the Tzimisce. This was the
presence they had felt in the mausoleum for so long, and never been able to name. It seemed to have
lain latent until now, torpid perhaps. This was the first time any of them had seen it take a form.

---

Richart reached past his senses and made sure. There was nothing else in the crypt, natural or
otherwise, besides the wight and themselves.

He had slept with his gun, and he woke with it. He took the safety off, took his time, and fired.
Both shots went home. The body was solid enough; the bullets went into it. They did not slow it at
all.

Nico held back. He wanted to wait until it was hurt, or distracted, and then speak to it. "We've
been hanging out for a while," he said, "and I finally get to see him, and he's being so cranky.
Don't eat my friends."

Claire let her blood thicken in her veins. Kamal called his blood up into his body, ready.

The wight came for Richart, because Richart had shot it. It clawed him. He could not keep it off.

Susanna went for it. She sprang onto its back, from behind, as it tore at Richart, and she sank her
teeth into it.

Nico spoke. "Stay still."

It heard him. It was not his, and it did not stop moving, but it did not attack.

Richart rolled out from under it. It swiped at him as he went and missed, and he reloaded.

Susanna drank. The hunger went down, and down, and the frenzy let her go. She was clear-headed
again, and could let go of the thing before it turned on her.

Kamal reached into its blood and choked it. From outside, it made no difference. The thing had
woken in a terrible hunger and the hunger drove it.

Nico had a blade on him. He sank it into the wight and pulled it out again. "Just listen next time,"
he told it, "and step back." Tough love.

Claire had a small knife. She went for its neck, and her cut went deep.

Richart fired again, and grazed it.

It turned on Claire and swiped at her, hard. She fell across its leg as she pulled back, and it came
down, and her femur broke. She rolled clear with one leg bent the wrong way.

Kamal came in from the side, low and quick, and latched on, and began to feed.

Nico tried again. "Sit down."

It sat. Kamal drank, and the body went slack, and fell back, and sank into torpor.

---

Richart looked around once more with his other sight. Nothing else.

He kicked the cooler of bagged blood across the floor to Claire.

Then he went to her. He took her knife and, like a surgeon, cut away her clothes around the break.
He lowered his mouth to her leg, and his tongue came out: not a human tongue, but six inches of it,
a foot, two feet, three, and it wound slowly round the injured leg. He could not heal the bone
outright. But he worked the flesh, and hurried it along, until the leg could take her weight.

Calliope, they realised, had gone into torpor too.

"So what exactly was that?"

"I think it's our friend, the one that's been around here for a while. Just hard to see."

"Well, I saw him coming out of the earth, which is something that not many people know how to do."

"He was much less bashful today. Assertive, even, as he tried to attack Calli."

Kamal could tell it was a strong vampire. Not nearly as strong as the one they had been guarding
here. Either it had been in torpor all this time and had only now woken, or it had been here all
along, keeping its head down, until it felt safe to come out or could not wait any longer.

Claire tasted its blood. A wight. She had heard the stories and had never seen one. When Kindred
spoke of keeping the Beast at bay, this was what they meant to keep at bay. It was what the
Camarilla built all its structures to prevent.

---

Susanna had come back to herself, and none of them had properly met her. They sat down to it.

"I'm sorry, who are you?"

"My name is Susanna."

"And what is she doing here?"

"We believe she was Baruch's guest," said Kamal, "the night before he died."

"And why were you not there when we first came?"

"I was captured and questioned. And I don't remember what went on that night."

The Camarilla, the Sabbat, the Anarchs: none of it meant much to her. She did not know who the
people who questioned her were. Only that they were mortal. Not vampires.

"If mortals had you, and yet here you are, why did they let you go?"

"I wish I knew. I think it might have been luck." She believed she had kept her third eye hidden
from them. "I didn't let them take too much of my blood. I tried to get away with telling them as
little as possible, and they let me go."

"And what is your purpose, now that you're here?"

"I don't know. I'm just trying to get my bearings and figure that out, because I'm new to all of
this. And who I was staying with, he's gone. And that sucks."

Claire interjected. She and Kamal had learned something about Susanna at Baruch's. She was
powerful, in some way Claire could not quite understand. Someone like that could be very useful
to a great many people: an asset, or someone who needed protecting, because of it.

Nico had a question. "How do you feel about boots?"

"Did you say boots?"

"I did. It's a very important topic."

"I love boots. The fancier the better."

"She's a useful asset for us to have around."

"And she was the first, after Calli, to jump into this scrap," said Richart.

---

Richart had news of his own. "While you were finding a new friend, Nico and I were talking to one
of my associates. We have passed on word of Baruch's passing to the upper echelons, and have been
told that we should present our information at the upcoming meeting."

"The Elysium."

Outside the mausoleum there was a dead drop. It had been used before, though not often. Tonight
there was a small sealed packet in it, to be delivered, and a note on top. Against all custom,
Richart was invited to the Elysium. It was tomorrow night. The packet was from Milica, for the
Prince, and the seal on it was a blood seal.

"We need to start watching the most recent YouTube videos," said Nico, "to figure out the
appropriate fashion to wear tomorrow."

Claire and Kamal knew that much. It was black tie. Fancy hors d'oeuvres.

Kamal had a question for Nico. "You're always talking about eyes in the walls. Are you seeing any
eyes right now?"

"No. Definitely no eyes."

"There were eyes last time we were at Baruch's, correct?"

"I don't know if the eyes were at Baruch's or if the eyes were in me. But I was seeing eyes all over
the place."

---

Richart went to look at the wight, medically. He was interested in how it differed from the rest of
them, and in the Tzimisce trick it had used. How long would it be out? Claire could answer that
from its blood: about a month, unless someone did something to bring it back sooner.

There were catacombs underneath the mausoleum, not large, not winding, with places that could be
more or less sealed. They would not hold it if it woke strong and meant to claw its way out. Nor
would anything else.

But a stake would. A stake would not kill it; it would hold it paralysed even as it came out of
torpor. And the mausoleum had one, behind glass on the wall, for emergencies. Richart broke the
glass with the blunt end of Claire's dagger, took the stake out, and with surgical precision drove
it home.

"I hope that wasn't a mistake."

"It is a mistake that can be undone. Unlike if it killed one of us."

---

The guest list, as far as they knew it: Prince Beatrice, and her seneschal, Gisele, were the two who
mattered. Javier would be there; he had been preparing the security. Claire knew her sire, Michel,
would be there. There were the regulars, and a few invited guests. It was strictly the Camarilla's,
with invitations extended to a select few others. The Sabbat were not generally welcome, which was
what made Richart's invitation notable; to the Camarilla he was still Sabbat, whatever ties he had
cut. The Malkavians were welcome as a clan, and so Nico was welcome. And Calliope had an invitation
too, though that had been Baruch pulling strings.

Calliope was still in torpor. Blood from a stronger vampire would bring her out of it sooner.

Kamal went to cut himself for her. "As much as you seem to like being hungry," said Richart,
"perhaps someone else should be doing that."

"I do owe her."

"You can owe her later. Nico, how generous are you feeling tonight?"

Nico fed her. She would wake in a night or two, rather than a month.

---

Claire looked properly at the freezer box Calliope had brought. There were fifteen or twenty bags
in it, and good for her. And they were plainly part of the blood that had been taken from the
hospital's missing truck. She would have liked to take it back, but there was no walking into a
hospital with bags of blood and saying you had found them by the road. Richart had storage. She
would keep it, for now, while she thought about her ethics.

Nico had a vision. Susanna, lying in darkness, the edges of the room glowing, and above her the
northern lights, shifting in and out of phase, and he could feel them and hear them. And when they
resolved he looked down again and saw human observers all around the room. Some wore vestments with
body armour over them. Some were soldiers. Some wore the badges of CSIS, Canadian
counter-intelligence. And one of the priest-like figures wore regalia he knew: the Society of St.
Leopold, an ancient order that had been part of the first Inquisition, hundreds of years ago.

He had been staring hard at one spot on the wall all through their talk. He scratched his head.

"The eyes have arms and badges!"

Then he turned round. "Sorry, what were you guys saying?"

---

Could anyone trace where Susanna had been held? Nobody could. Whoever had had her had been
thorough.

Kamal had a hunch about Nico's eyes and Baruch's cameras. The cameras had been streaming; there was
nothing stored on them to go back to. Javier had them. It was a cold trail.

Richart had one more idea. He could take recent memories away. Perhaps, done the other way, it
might shake something loose. He explained it to Susanna, and she let him try. There was nothing
there. It was not that she was hiding it, or holding it back. She had simply not been conscious for
any of it.

Nico was walking along the walls, stopping at one spot for a while, then moving on and doing it
again somewhere else, poking at it.

---

Kamal had one tool left, and he did not like it. He could walk Susanna's blood. It might show them
any bonds she carried. It would take about three hours, and there were risks, for him most of all:
that blood could send any Kindred into frenzy.

"Why don't you explain these risks," said Claire.

"Must I?"

They talked it through around Susanna, and she broke in. "I'm confused, and I want to know what this
blood walk is, and what we're hoping to accomplish with it."

It would show her lineage, Kamal said, and any blood bonds she had, whether she knew of them or not.

She knew her lineage. Her sire was Estelle. And she had decided to trust them. Estelle had been one
of the last of the Salubri, and so was she. Estelle had been mentoring her, meaning to pass her role
on to Susanna, and had been destroyed before she could, and now Susanna did not know what she was
meant to be doing. She had been learning Golconda from her: how to tame her own hunger, how to make
peace with the Beast within.

Claire looked very confused. It was nothing like what she had been taught. She kept it to herself.

Did Susanna want to know who else she was connected to? She did.

---

Nico was talking to the walls, whispering, arguing sometimes, looking over his shoulder.

Richart went over to him. "Nico, thank you for all your intervention in the fight. And as a more
physical thank you, tomorrow night I will wear whatever cufflinks you wish. You have a budget of
three hundred dollars to choose, or buy. Go nuts."

It took Nico nearly until Richart was back with the others to answer. "Wonderful! I can't wait, this
is going to be so much fun." Then he went back to poking the wall and whispering. The word they
could make out was *north*.

Susanna had not known him long. She asked about his boot collection, and whether he had a special
pair of dress boots for the event.

He did. Thigh-high on the right, ankle-high on the left; he loved the asymmetry. He was not sure
boots were still in for people as young as he was. He would have to check YouTube. "But thank you so
much for — north, north, north — for asking."

"Maybe I can get some asymmetrical boots for my dog," said Susanna.

Nico went back to walking in circles inside his vision.

---

Kamal would do it alone, in a room where he could hurt nobody. Richart offered something first.

"I can make it so you will be a little less physically strong, in order to break down doors, but a
bit more mentally strong, to resist whatever this blood may do to you. Is that something you are
interested in?"

"I would accept that help. Thank you."

"Have a seat."

Kamal sat. Richart came round behind him, and Kamal felt hands on his shoulders, and smelled decay
and rotting flesh, and something wet and cold slid up his neck to his ear. Richart took the quickness
out of his body and gave it to his will. And then he took the strength out of his body altogether,
so that if the blood took him and he went for the doors, he would be no more than a kitten. It felt
like being very drunk, the limbs heavy and not quite his.

Claire drew more of Susanna's blood and brought it to him.

Kamal braced himself, and drank, and this time he did not merely resist. He beat it so completely
that her blood would never have that hold on him again.

The walk showed him what Susanna had already told them, and two things more. Not one blood bond but
two, and neither of them to her sire; her sire was dead. One was to an older man. He had no name for
him, only a picture. Tzimisce, like Richart. Close, and recent. The other was a thin connection, far
off, and ran the other way: someone was bound to her.

---

When it was done Kamal crawled across the floor, as best he could, and called for Susanna. Richart
went in with her. He did not trust her alone in a room with Kamal.

"Susanna, there is an older man, who is Tzimisce. In the blood walk I saw one regnant, and one thrall.
So who have you been feeding blood to, and whose blood has been fed to you?"

She knew of no one but Estelle.

"It's an older man. Not Baruch's face."

"No. Maybe something happened during that time that I can't remember. Maybe the humans fed me blood.
I don't know about that."

"Why were you at Baruch's? Was there some ritual that was meant to occur?"

Estelle had been finishing her siring there. She had business, and Baruch had been making
connections on their behalf, for a relocation.

"Is it common for there to be a relationship with more than one older Kindred?"

"No. There shouldn't be. That's what's weird about this."
