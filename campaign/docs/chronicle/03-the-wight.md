---
title: The Wight
part: Friday, 11 June 2021
---

Susanna woke in a hunger frenzy.

The rest of them woke to find Calliope squared off against a wight. It had come up out of the
soil, roaring, and it went for her. They fought it, and it tore into Calliope and put her down,
into torpor.

This was the haunting, then, the presence they had been feeling in the crypt, given a body.
Richart was sure it was alone. Perhaps it had lain latent until now, or in torpor.

Nico was entirely calm about it, and tried to reason with it. Kamal called up his blood. Claire
readied hers. The wight turned on Richart and went for him.

Susanna steered herself at it. She threw herself onto its back and sank her teeth into its neck,
and drank, and the hunger eased, and the frenzy let go of her.

Nico told it to be still, and it was.

Kamal reached into it and choked the blood in its body, and still it fought, and fought hard.

Richart drew his handgun and fired. The bullets buried themselves in its flesh.

The wight went for Claire. She tried to pull away, and it came down on her, and her leg bent in a
direction legs do not go.

Nico was disappointed that it had not listened. "Sit!"

Kamal jumped on it and latched on to feed as well. The body went slack and sank into torpor.

---

Richart looked at Claire. He took her knife and cut the leg of her trousers away, and his tongue
reached out to the leg, and under it the flesh moved and set into a shape that would carry her
weight again.

"So what exactly was that?" Richart said.

"Our bashful friend finally made an appearance," said Nico.

"It came from the earth," said Richart. "A distinctly Tzimisce approach, if I do say so."

Kamal could tell it was a strong vampire, though not as strong as the one who had lain here under
guard. Either it had only just come out of torpor, or it had been hiding a long time.

Claire tasted its blood. It was what she had been warned of: what a Kindred becomes when the Beast
takes them wholly. It would be out for about a month.

---

They caught each other up. Susanna was introduced to Richart and Nico. She belonged to no
faction, she said. Mortals had taken her, and then let her go. She believed she had kept her third
eye hidden from them, but she had had to give them some of her blood.

Claire spoke up. She thought Susanna's blood was powerful. That she might be an asset, or else
someone in need of protecting.

"How do you feel about boots?" Nico asked her.

"The more boots, the better."

"A useful asset."

"She was first after Calli to jump into the scrap, as well," said Richart.

Richart told them he had passed the news of the attack on Baruch's to his contacts. Nico began
researching fashion for Elysium. Kamal thought the eyes Nico had been seeing might have been the
cameras Javier found in Baruch's haven.

Richart looked the wight's body over. He fetched a stake he had hidden away for just this kind of
emergency, and staked it, to be safe.

Kamal went to feed Calliope some of his own vitae to help her wake. Richart stopped him, and had
Nico do it instead. It would take her a couple of nights to come fully back.

Claire noticed that the bags of blood Calliope had brought had been meant for the hospital.

---

Nico had a vision. Susanna, lying in the dark, a glow all around her, looking up, and above her
the northern lights. Watchers stood all around: some dressed as priests, some in fatigues, some in
suits. One of the men wore the regalia of the Society of St. Leopold, which went back to the first
Inquisition.

"The eyes have arms and badges!" Nico screamed, staring at nothing on the wall, stroking it.

Richart asked Susanna whether he might reach into her memory and try to bring back anything she
had lost. He tried. There was nothing there to bring back.

---

Kamal wanted to walk Susanna's blood, to learn whether she carried any bonds. He laid out the
risks, frenzy above all. Claire, Richart and Kamal began to talk through how it would be done, and
Susanna broke in, confused.

To earn their trust, she told them. She was Salubri. Estelle had been her sire. She was willing to
give a little more blood, safely, if it would help untangle what had happened.

Claire was surprised. It was nothing like what the Tremere had taught her about the Salubri. She
said nothing.

Nico was still hallucinating at the walls. Susanna asked him about his boots, and whether he had
a special pair for the event. That got his attention. He described a pair of asymmetrical boots,
and then went back to walking in circles inside his vision. Richart gave him three hundred dollars
to buy a pair of cufflinks fit for Elysium.

---

Kamal and Richart went off by themselves to do it. First Richart sat Kamal down and put his
rotting hands on Kamal's shoulders, and his tongue ran down Kamal's neck and up to his ear, and
under it he took some of the quickness out of Kamal's body and gave it to his will.

Claire had drawn more of Susanna's blood. She brought it to Kamal with a chalice.

Kamal drank. He braced against the frenzy, and this time he beat it, and beat it for good: her
blood would not have that hold on him again.

The walk began. He felt an older Kindred, Tzimisce, close and recent. And another thing bound to
Susanna, but further off.

When it was done he called Susanna in, and Richart came too. Kamal told her what he had seen.

"One regnant, and one thrall."

Susanna knew nothing of either bond.

---

Outside the mausoleum, someone had left a sealed packet for Richart in a dead drop, and a note
with it. He had an invitation. He was to attend the Elysium the following night.
