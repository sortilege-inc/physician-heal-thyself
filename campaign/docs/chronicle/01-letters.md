---
title: Letters
part: Wednesday, 9 June 2021
---

The letters came in Baruch Espinosa's own hand, one to each of them, and none of them said the
same thing.

To Claire:

> Our esteemed Warlock
>
> I believe it will be to your delight that I introduce my latest acquisitions; the seventh, and the ninth that precedes the eighth. Do come and witness my latest acquisition, and sharpen your knives.

To Calliope:

> My intemperate Promethea,
>
> These nights see us poised upon a knife's edge. Our city lacks vision, and is fraught with the squabbles of the leaderless. This detente cannot hold, and when the opportunity arises, power must be grasped, swiftly and without remorse. I care not the face of a new order, for I have seen the rise and fall of Anarch, Camarilla, and Sabbat, each in turn. Use the tool you find most pliant.
>
> For now, I caution patience. Be ye the virgin with oil for the night, vigilant to seize upon the bridegroom, and to take his place to consummate influence.
>
> Watchfully yours, Baruch.

To Richart:

> My dear ancilla
>
> In these modern nights, one must assume all discrete communications lack discretion, and that any apparent cryptogram must elicit a remedy, even years hence. As such, and having learned from the failures of past systems, I have found myself enamored with the arts and sciences of steganography. When one reads the surface and is contented, the esoteric meanings may be relayed without danger or harm.
>
> To this end, may I advise that you watch the fortunes of the Dynamo ARSQ this season, in our city's Chronicle-Telegraph.

To Kamal:

> To our austere Assamite,
>
> A Spaniard ductus walks carelessly through these streets. It would be tragic if the steel trap of a kind of justice were to snap upon him, and he be waylaid.
>
> His blood is old and strong and, while I am no sommelier, carries certain notes that you may be able to appreciate.
>
> Yours, Baruch Espinosa

To Nico:

> An absent-minded lech such as yourself may notice that histories, both personal and societal, suffer themes and variations. The Levant harkens, leaving a vacuum to be abhorred à la débandade. Surely an oracle such as yourself sees the opportunity in this. It would please me greatly to consult.
>
> Yours truly, Baruch

---

They fed first.

Nico fed from a woman he met out on the town, who had been drinking. Claire
took hers from a blood bag at the hospital, which was the kind of thing a night nurse could do.
Richart went to the hospital too, and fed from a patient who slept through it. Calliope took
someone on the way over.

Kamal did not feed at all. He arrived on time and well groomed, and his stigmata was showing.

---

Nico got there first and hid, to wait for Calliope.

Richart and Claire arrived together. Richart saw it before they reached the door: the entrance
had not been hidden. Baruch always hid the entrance. They went in anyway, and Kamal came in close
behind them.

Richart reached past his ordinary senses into the dark ahead and felt the dead there. Recently
dead. He texted the others. Most of them carried burners. Richart carried a smartphone.

Calliope walked straight up to where Nico was hiding.

"You should learn to hide better," she said, and they went in together.

---

The door to the haven had been broken in with a ram, or something that did a ram's work.

Inside, the filing cabinets had been tipped over and gone through. Computers and electronics had
been ripped out of the walls and taken. And there were two husks on the floor that had been
vampires, beheaded and burned.

Claire went to the files. Baruch had kept information on everybody, most of it banal, and there
was a great deal of it, and a great many Kindred named in it. She found full files on Kamal and on
Richart. Partial files on Calliope, and on herself. Nothing on Nico.

Kamal found splattered vitae and tasted it. Two vampires, both older than him: one strong, one a
good deal stronger. He could not say whether they were the two on the floor or two others. If one
of them was Baruch, it was the weaker.

Claire looked closer at one of the bodies. Something on its face had melted. It might have been a
mask. It might have been Baruch's mask, his signature, out of *The Phantom of the Opera*.

---

Nico wandered off down the hall to the guest room. There had been a struggle there. Blood was
thrown across the wall and the floor, and Nico cocked his head and stared into it for some time.

Kamal came in behind him and tasted this blood too. A third Kindred, and a mortal. The Kindred's
blood was the most delicious thing he had ever tasted.

An argument over artistic differences, Nico decided. Probably.

Kamal went to find Claire.

---

Richart went into the second room. It was open, but nobody had ransacked it. The built-in
shelves were full of old things wrapped in newspaper. He took two of the wrapped curios, to find
out later what they were.

Calliope went through the broken door of the last room. A four-poster bed, where Baruch had
presumably rested. A wardrobe of suits. A small space for meditation, and somebody had used it
recently. She called Kamal in and let him feed from her.

Claire kept reading. She found a dossier on the Tremere, and took it with her.

---

Baruch had been fastidious about his security. If he had died in his own haven, it was a failure
of immense proportions.

Calliope turned to Nico. "Why don't you have a file?"

Nico was as angry about it as she was. He wanted to see what pictures Baruch had kept of him.

Somebody, or something, had truly killed two powerful vampires in this house, and something had
happened to a third. And Baruch had collected far more than any of them had realised.
