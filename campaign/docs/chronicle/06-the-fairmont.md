---
title: The Fairmont
part: Monday, 14 June 2021
---

Nico, Claire and Susanna woke to find Calliope still in torpor, and Richart and Kamal gone.

Claire took Susanna aside to talk in private. She proposed an alliance, just the two of them. If
Susanna would give her vitae, there was a ritual Claire had been learning that could overwhelm
any enemy or threat. She admitted she had never quite managed it before. Susanna's blood, she
believed, would make the difference.

Susanna wanted to hold off on any arrangement until Jerome's contact arrived. Claire was shocked.
Susanna had already made a deal with Jerome.

Jerome could not be trusted, Nico said. He poured weak drinks.

---

Matthieu arrived with several other bikers, to collect Susanna as promised. She asked for a little
time. Matthieu left one of his bikers outside to bring her along when she was ready.

He asked whether they had seen Calliope. She owed him for some bagged blood. She was in torpor,
they told him. He took the blood back, and left. Claire's eyes followed the cooler out of the
door.

Nico asked Susanna what she had offered Jerome.

"A major boon," she said. For a safe haven.

Nico turned to Claire. "We should get her to stay."

"I'm confused," said Susanna. "Why would I need to stay to be protected?"

Claire's answer confused her more. But Claire talked her into staying with them a little while,
and into giving some blood so Claire could attempt the ritual.

Susanna wanted a rider on it, a magical one: if either of them betrayed the other, they would be
struck down into torpor. Claire agreed in principle. She did not know how to do such a thing.
Michel might. A blood bond might serve, but that Claire could not do.

---

There was a disturbance outside. Javier and Michel came in.

Claire looked surprised. "What do you need?"

Michel looked around the room. "By process of elimination, I take it she's the one?"

Claire tried to lie, and it went terribly. Michel glared at her. What kind of stunt was she trying
to pull?

"I'm here because of the attempted Sabbat ambush. How did they get those coordinates? The ones
you gave me were for Beatrice's haven."

Claire pointed at Nico.

"How do they know where the Prince sleeps?"

Nico shrugged.

"Well, you're going to help us hit them back."

"That doesn't sound like fun, no thanks."

Michel bared his teeth. "I wasn't asking."

Kamal came back in. "We know where AURORA is going to hit next, we need to help move them move
out."

Kamal, Michel and Javier went out.

---

Claire slumped. "He knows who you are now, Susanna, and Michel is dangerous."

Nico bought them some time. He hung a sock on the outside doorknob and locked the door from the
inside.

Claire spent an hour preparing the ritual, with Susanna's blood mixed into the bagged blood, and
worked it on herself. Susanna said she trusted her.

Then Susanna understood what she had agreed to. For now, she depended entirely on Claire for her
protection, and that upset her.

Susanna left first, with the Gangrel biker, for Matthieu's haven.

---

Claire and Nico went out after her. Kamal told them she had gone, and took them to the Fairmont,
where Nico's coordinates led. He brought them in by a side entrance, and they moved through it
quietly.

In a room off the corridor, Gisele, Javier and Michel were watching security camera footage. On
the screens were Glaurung and several other Sabbat, at least, armed.

"Come in," said Javier, "action is about to start."

Michel turned to Claire. "Where is she?"

"Maybe another time? I didn't want to ruin this moment."

"No. Where is she?"

"Jerome. She made a deal with Jerome."

Michel was stunned, and then he lashed out. "You gave the Salubri to the Anarchs?"

"She sort of ran off. We..."

"You let her run away?"

"She's her own person, man," said Nico.

"No, she is a tool, and she's ours to be wielded."

"If she were, she would be here."

"If my childer were competent, that would be true."

"Sounds like you're a bad parent," Nico said.

"We'll deal with you after."

---

On the screens, the lights went out. Beams of light swung around in the dark, soldiers coming in,
muzzle flashes, and it was hard to tell what was happening. They could hear that it was happening
just down the hall.

Javier stood. "Let's ensure no one gets out."

He and Kamal ran down the hallway. Claire stayed back with Nico.

"Do you want me to go retrieve her now?" she asked Michel.

"We need to decapitate the Sabbat while we can, but afterwards I need you to chase her down. We
cannot allow a prize like that to get away."

Claire went down the hall.

A couple of soldiers burst out of the room ahead, terrified, and ran. Behind them came a roar, and
the wight, and it ran them down and tore them limb from limb. Nico heard the roar and jumped up and
ran after it. Kamal and Claire took the chance and slipped into the room.

---

The room was wrecked, and the stench of blood was thick. There were figures in the shadows all
around them. Claire caught sight of Sybille at the edge of her vision and wished she had not come.
She froze. Then she screamed and began to back away, and backed into someone.

Two hands took hold of her shoulders. She turned, and it was Richart, and she breathed out.

The hands were real, though. Not rotting any more. Not sewn on.

"What's going on Richart?"

Richart grinned. "The Camarilla thought we had set a trap for them, but we knew they would send
their human agents in their stead. Do you realize the trouble your friends have been causing, in
breach of their beloved masquerade?"

Claire pushed back. "You've been using AURORA to target havens across the city; Baruch?"

"It wasn't the Sabbat. Why would we stoop to using a vessel like that?"

Kamal and Javier had seen how badly outnumbered they were, and were giving themselves up to the
Tzimisce, who shaped restraints for them out of flesh.

---

Down the hall, Nico caught up with the wight. It was chewing on a soldier.

"Good doggy! Good doggy!"

He picked up a severed arm and threw it back down the hall, and tried to get it to fetch.

---

"Were you aware of any of this?" Claire asked Kamal.

Kamal turned to Javier. "It seems we were told what we needed for our own protection."

Claire asked Richart how he could betray his coterie. The Sabbat had given him back his hands, he
said, and a position of authority.

Claire's arms caught fire.

Sybille hissed at the flames and drew back, and Richart backed away. Claire raised both arms, and
Milica was gibbering with terror. Kamal kept his eyes carefully down. Glaurung was wary. Javier was
on the floor, convulsing.

Claire tried to get Kamal to help her. He showed her his hands, bound in flesh, and asked her to cover
him while he ran for help.

---

The wight came back with the arm. "Good doggy," Nico said, and held it with his eyes, and then the
screaming started in the room and he saw Kamal come out. Nico and the wight followed Kamal back to
Michel and Gisele.

"We need to help Claire and Javier," said Kamal, "the Sabbat have them!"

Gisele and Michel argued that they should get out now, and come back with reinforcements.

Nico told the wight to attack Michel. It did not do much damage. It got Michel's attention, though,
when Nico called it back to heel.

"That thing's yours?"

"It is. We have things to do. Will you play nicely?"

"We need to get out."

"We have folks to help. Be useful."

Michel looked at him. "Be my guest."

---

Claire saw that Glaurung had a gun on her. She dove for his ankles with her arms ablaze. Both of
them missed: he fired as he jumped back.

Nico arrived. "Need anything?"

"Thank god," said Claire, and pointed at Glaurung.

Glaurung glared at Nico. "Get her out of here, she's insane."

He fired at Claire again, and this time he hit her. Nico set the wight on him. When the gun went
flying, Nico called it off.

"What are your intentions here?" Glaurung asked him.

"What would you like to do next, Claire?"

Claire had got up and backed away to Nico's side. "We need to get out of here, we need to get
Javier up."

"We need to deal with them decisively," said Kamal. "We cannot allow a rot like this to fester."

Nico had the wight pick Javier up. Then he spoke to all of the Sabbat. "If we let you go, I need a
favor."

Richart chided him. "They won't owe you anything if they allow you to leave without the final
death. If Claire and Kamal want to be idiots that's on them, not you."

Nico pointed at Claire's arms. "You see those? If we leave, they owe us."

Richart looked at Milica, and back, and nodded.

"Fine," said Glaurung. "If you all leave."

Claire looked at Kamal, doubtful, and backed out through the door. Kamal went last, resigned, once
it was clear neither of his allies was staying.
