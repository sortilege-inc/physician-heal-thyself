Letters from Baruch

To Claire:

Our esteemed Warlock
I believe it will be to your delight that I introduce my latest acquisitions; the seventh, and the ninth that precedes the eighth. Do come and witness my latest acquisition, and sharpen your knives.

To Calliope:

My intemperate Promethea,
These nights see us poised upon a knife's edge. Our city lacks vision, and is fraught with the squabbles of the leaderless. This detente cannot hold, and when the opportunity arises, power must be grasped, swiftly and without remorse. I care not the face of a new order, for I have seen the rise and fall of Anarch, Camarilla, and Sabbat, each in turn. Use the tool you find most pliant.
For now, I caution patience. Be ye the virgin with oil for the night, vigilant to seize upon the bridegroom, and to take his place to consummate influence.
Watchfully yours, Baruch.

To Richart:

My dear ancilla
In these modern nights, one must assume all discrete communications lack discretion, and that any apparent cryptogram must elicit a remedy, even years hence. As such, and having learned from the failures of past systems, I have found myself enamored with the arts and sciences of steganography. When one reads the surface and is contented, the esoteric meanings may be relayed without danger or harm.
To this end, may I advise that you watch the fortunes of the Dynamo ARSQ this season, in our city's Chronicle-Telegraph.

To Kamal:

To our austere Assamite,
A Spaniard ductus walks carelessly through these streets. It would be tragic if the steel trap of a kind of justice were to snap upon him, and he be waylaid.
His blood is old and strong and, while I am no sommelier, carries certain notes that you may be able to appreciate.
Yours, Baruch Espinosa

To Nico:

An absent-minded lech such as yourself may notice that histories, both personal and societal, suffer themes and variations. The Levant harkens, leaving a vacuum to be abhorred à la débandade. Surely an oracle such as yourself sees the opportunity in this. It would please me greatly to consult.
Yours truly, Baruch

Baruch's Haven

PCs: Calliope, Richart, Nico, Claire, Kamal

Baruch's Haven.

Nico hides in wait for Calli.

Richart and Claire arrive. Richart notices that the care to conceal the entrance hasn't been taken. They head inside. Kamal arrives shortly after and goes inside.

Richart uses Sense the Unseen and gets a feeling that there are recent dead ahead. Sends a text to the group chat (mostly burner phones, except Richart who uses a smart phone).

Calli walks up and nonchalantly tells Nico "You should learn to hide better." They head inside.

The door to Baruch's haven has been broken open with a battering ram or similar.

Filing cabinets have been knocked over, gone through; there are two husks of vampires, beheaded and burned. It looks like some computers and other electronics were ripped out of the walls and removed. Claire looks through the files, realizes that Baruch had information on everybody; a lot of it seemingly banal, but there are a lot of kindred referenced here. She finds full files on Kamal and Richart, partial files for Calli and herself, and nothing on Nico. Kamal finds some splattered vitae and A Taste of Blood reveals two vampires, older (blood potency 3 and 5 or 6). They are unsure whether they are the deceased or others; the weaker of the two could presumably be Baruch. Claire notices that one of the bodies appears to have a melted mask; perhaps Baruch's signature Phantom of the Opera mask.

Nico wanders down into the hall and into the guest room. There is signs of a struggle, and blood splattered across wall and floor. Nico cocks his head and stares into it. Kamal walks in and performs A Taste of Blood again, identifying a third kindred and a mortal. The kindred blood is the most delicious he has ever had. Nico speculates that the scene was the result of an argument over artistic differences, likely. Kamal confers with Claire.

Richart moves down and enters the second room. It is opened but doesn't appear ransacked. There are all kinds of older items wrapped in newspaper and held in built-in shelves. He removes two wrapped curios to identify later.

Calli proceeds to last room, through the broken door. A four-poster bed is presumably where Baruch rested, and a wardrobe holds various suits. There's a small meditation space that appears to have been used recently. She calls Kamal into the room and allows him to feed from her (+1 Hunger for Calli, -1 for Kamal).

Claire continues to peruse the files, and finds a dossier on the Tremere, and takes it for review.

Baruch was fastidious about personal security; if he died in his own haven, that is a security failure of immense proportions.

Calli turns to Nico: "Why don't you have a file?". Nico is also incensed, he wants to review what pictures he had of him.

Takeaways:

  • It is likely that someone or something truly killed two powerful vampires, and something happened to a third.
  • Baruch had far more information collected than anyone realized.
