Letters from Baruch Espinosa

Tremere

Our esteemed Warlock

I believe it will be to your delight that I introduce my latest acquisitions; the seventh, and the ninth that precedes the eighth. Do come and witness my latest acquisition, and sharpen your knives.

Brujah

My intemperate Promethea,

These nights see us poised upon a knife's edge. Our city lacks vision, and is fraught with the squabbles of the leaderless. This detente cannot hold, and when the opportunity arises, power must be grasped, swiftly and without remorse. I care not the face of a new order, for I have seen the rise and fall of Anarch, Camarilla, and Sabbat, each in turn. Use the tool you find most pliant.

For now, I caution patience. Be ye the virgin with oil for the night, vigilant to seize upon the bridegroom, and to take his place to consummate influence.

Watchfully yours, Baruch.

Tzimisce

My dear ancilla

In these modern nights, one must assume all discrete communications lack discretion, and that any apparent cryptogram must elicit a remedy, even years hence. As such, and having learned from the failures of past systems, I have found myself enamored with the arts and sciences of steganography. When one reads the surface and is contented, the esoteric meanings may be relayed without danger or harm.

To this end, may I advise that you watch the fortunes of the Dynamo ARSQ this season, in our city's Chronicle-Telegraph.

Banu Haqim

To our austere Assamite,

A Spaniard ductus walks carelessly through these streets. It would be tragic if the steel trap of a kind of justice were to snap upon him, and he be waylaid.

His blood is old and strong and, while I am no sommelier, carries certain notes that you may be able to appreciate.

Yours, Baruch Espinosa

Malkavian

An absent-minded lech such as yourself may notice that histories, both personal and societal, suffer themes and variations. The Levant harkens, leaving a vacuum to be abhorred à la débandade. Surely an oracle such as yourself sees the opportunity in this. It would please me greatly to consult.

Yours truly, Baruch
